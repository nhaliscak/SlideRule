# Code of conduct

SlideRule is a small project that welcomes students, hobbyists and professional engineers. We want it to be a place where people can ask basic
questions, point out mistakes (including the maintainers') and disagree without it turning personal. This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) (CC BY 4.0).

## What we expect

* Be respectful. Criticise code, results and ideas, not people.
* Assume good faith and say what you did and saw, so others can help.
* Welcome beginners; a question about units or a formula is never "too basic".
* Credit other people's work, and do not paste copyrighted tables, standards or book pages into issues or pull requests.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, sexualised comments, sustained disruption of
discussions, and anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project in public. The maintainers may edit
or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, or
contact the maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/nhaliscak. Reports are handled privately, and the person who reported is not named to the
person reported without their consent.
