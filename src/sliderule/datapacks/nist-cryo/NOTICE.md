# NIST cryogenic material properties (derived data)

Source: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), Cryogenic Technologies Group, Cryogenic Material Properties, https://trc.nist.gov/cryogenics/materials/materialproperties.htm. Terms of use: https://www.nist.gov/open/license.

## Change notice

Changed by SlideRule: NIST publishes polynomial fit coefficients, not tables. SlideRule evaluated each fit at 60 log-spaced temperatures inside the range covered by both NIST's data and its equation, rounded the results to 6 significant figures and stores them as a curve that is interpolated between those points (PCHIP). Only thermal conductivity and specific heat are used. Built 2026-11-05.
